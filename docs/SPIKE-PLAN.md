# Measurement spike plan

One day, after Kurt's decisions of 2026-10-21 ([DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md): architecture B from the start, the shared `lgu-lights` Earth Engine service account, no published dataset). Items 1–12 are the original plan; items 13–18 were added the same day for the Java slice, the shared Earth Engine quota and the milestone-1 sources from the canvas ([DATA-PLAN.md](DATA-PLAN.md) §9). Throwaway code in `spike/` (outputs gitignored); results go to `SPIKE-RESULTS.md` as a table (measurement, estimate, result, verdict) and an addendum S1…Sn in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). The spike never backfills, never creates permanent resources, never registers on the PHIVOLCS portal, and keeps Earth Engine use to a few EECU-minutes.

## What the spike does not do

- No full backfill, no crawl beyond about 100 pages, no Earth Engine run beyond about 30 minutes of batch time (and under 2 EECU-hours of the shared `lgu-lights` quota).
- The Render service and the Neon branch created for items 13–15 are deleted at the end of the day; nothing stays deployed. Only `plan: free` resources are created, and the item-15 loop stays under 100 MB of origin egress.
- No registration on PHIVOLCS-LAVA, HazardHunterPH or GeoRiskPH; no FIRMS archive request beyond one small email download if item 3 needs `type`.
- No permanent deployment, no DNS, no repos, no datasets.
- No full test suites: targeted checks only.
